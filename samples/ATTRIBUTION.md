# Chordloom instrument recordings

- **Grand piano:** Salamander Grand Piano / Yamaha C5, Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0. Seventeen unmodified MP3 recordings from [Tonejs/audio](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander). See [full piano credit](piano/ATTRIBUTION.md).
- **Finger bass:** D. Smolken / Karoryfer, [Black And Blue Basses](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses), darkblack regular medium-force recordings. Two alternating takes at B1, E2, A2, D3 and G3. Mono conversion, 16-bit encoding, 2.8-second crops, normalization and end fades. [Publisher CC0 declaration](https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-samples), checked October 8, 2026.
- **Marimba:** Sam Gossner / Versilian Community Sample Library, [VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), medium-force Outrigger recordings. Source filenames G2, F3, C4, B4, F5 and C6 use C3-as-middle-C; their scientific pitches are G3, F4, C5, B5, F6 and C7. Tuning corrected to equal temperament. Mono conversion, 16-bit encoding, normalization and end fades. CC0 1.0.
- **Atlanta punch and long slide 808:** Original Chordloom processing of the Michael Fischer TR-808 BD0075 recording from [TidalCycles](https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer). Hardware transient followed by a periodically extended sampled waveform, tuned to C2 (65.406391 Hz), saturation, filtering and different decay contours. CC0 1.0. These are original processed sounds inspired by familiar trap production, not branded Spinz or Zay producer samples.
- Electric keys, velvet pad, prism bells, round sub, legacy warm 808 and log drum are synthesized by the app.

[CC0 license](CC0-LICENSE.txt). Exact upstream file URLs, transformations and SHA-256 checksums are in [PALETTE-MANIFEST.json](PALETTE-MANIFEST.json). The reproducible preparation script is `scripts/prepare-palette.py`. No commercial sample packs have been redistributed. MIDI transfers notes/velocity/tempo; WAV captures the actual sampled/synthesized timbres and slides.
